# JOCRAY Brand Logotypes

This folder contains the official logotypes and symbols for the JOCRAY media
outlet. These assets represent our brand identity across live streams,
tournaments, and social media platforms.

## Asset Varieties

- **Symbol (`JOCRAY_symbol.png`)**: Our standalone brand icon, perfect for
  profile pictures, favicons, and compact spaces.
- **Text (`JOCRAY_text.png`)**: The stylized "JOCRAY" wordmark used when the
  brand name needs to be clean and prominent.
- **Combined (`JOCRAY_symbol_text.png`)**: The primary combination of our
  symbol and text, recommended for banners, official documents, and main
  stream overlays.

## Usage Rules

These rules apply to all varieties of the logotype. They are referenced by
section 3 ("Brand Integrity") of the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file.

### Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to **0.5× the height of the symbol** on
all sides of any logotype. No text, graphics, or edges may enter this zone.

### Minimum Size

| Variety | Digital | Print |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Symbol | 24 px | 8 mm |
| Wordmark | 120 px | 30 mm |
| Combined logotype | 160 px | 40 mm |

Below these sizes the logotype loses legibility and must not be used.

### Approved Backgrounds

- White (`#ffffff`)
- JOCRAY Amethyst (`#6b21a8`)
- JOCRAY Lilac (`#e9d5ff`)

Do not place logotypes on busy imagery or on backgrounds with insufficient
contrast.

### Prohibited Modifications

Do not stretch, skew, rotate, recolor, add shadows or outlines, or rebuild the
logotype from the typeface. Only the files provided in this directory may be
used. The symbol, wordmark, and combined logotype are not interchangeable —
choose the variety appropriate to the context.
