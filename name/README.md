# About the JOCRAY Name

The name **JOCRAY** is at the heart of our independent digital media outlet. It stands for the intersection of gaming excitement, tournament competition, and community engagement. 

## Meaning and Concept
Derived to evoke a sense of energy and brightness, JOCRAY reflects our mission: creating experiences that bring pure joy to gamers and viewers worldwide. It represents independence, bold ideas, and high-quality gaming entertainment.

## Proper Representation
- Always capitalize the full name: **JOCRAY** (not `Jocray` or `jocray`), except in code identifiers and technical tokens where lowercase is required by the language or tooling.
- Used as a standalone noun or as a modifier (e.g., *JOCRAY Media*, *JOCRAY Tournaments*).
