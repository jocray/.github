# About the JOCRAY Name

The name **JOCRAY** lies at the heart of our independent digital media resource. It symbolizes the intersection of the excitement found in video games and active engagement within the community.

## Meaning and Concept
The name JOCRAY, designed to evoke a sense of energy and vibrancy, reflects our mission: to create experiences that bring pure joy to gamers and viewers around the world.

## Proper Representation
- The full name is always written in capital letters: **JOCRAY** (not `Jocray` or `jocray`); the only exceptions are identifiers in code and technical tokens, where the programming language or toolkit requires the use of lowercase letters.
- It is used either as a standalone noun or as a modifier (e.g., *JOCRAY Media*).
